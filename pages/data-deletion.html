Data Deletion

RenewWise stores your reminders, documents, and settings on your device. You can delete your data at any time using the options below.

Delete data in the app

Open Settings in RenewWise and use Delete All Data to permanently remove:

  • Your RenewWise account identifiers stored on the device (welcome login email and provider)
  • Reminders, history, expenses, goals, and assistant drafts
  • Stored documents in the vault and attached files on the device
  • Local app preferences and optional local backup cache

This action is performed on your device and cannot be undone.

Google Drive backup (if enabled)

If you connected Google Drive backup, encrypted backup files may remain in your Google Drive until you remove them.

  • In RenewWise, disconnect backup and clear local backup cache from Settings when deleting app data.
  • In Google Drive, delete files in your RenewWise backup folder, or revoke RenewWise access under your Google Account security settings.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app or need help deleting optional cloud backup files, email us at support.renewwise@gmail.com from the address linked to your RenewWise account.

Include:

  • The Google account email used with RenewWise (if applicable)
  • Whether Google Drive backup was enabled
  • What you want deleted (account identifiers, Drive backups, or all associated data)

We will confirm receipt and guide you through any remaining steps.

Uninstalling the app

Removing RenewWise from your device deletes local app data stored in the app sandbox. It does not automatically delete files you created separately in Google Drive.
